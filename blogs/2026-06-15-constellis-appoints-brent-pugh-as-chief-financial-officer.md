Herndon, Va. – June 15, 2026 – Constellis Holdings, LLC (“Constellis”), a global leader in risk management and mission-critical support services, today announced the appointments of Brent A. Pugh as Chief Financial Officer and Tarun Sondhi as Chief Growth & Technology Officer.
